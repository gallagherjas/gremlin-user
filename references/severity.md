# Severity

Classify confirmed defects by user and system impact.

## Critical

Use Critical when the defect can cause a real charge, irreversible data loss, cross-tenant exposure, privilege bypass, or a production-wide outage in the affected path.

## High

Use High when the defect can duplicate or corrupt important business data, block a core flow for many users, or allow an unauthorized action with limited scope.

## Medium

Use Medium when the defect breaks a recoverable workflow, loses unsaved work, creates confusing stale state, or requires a workaround that a user can discover.

## Low

Use Low when the defect has limited impact, preserves the underlying data, and has a clear workaround.

## Notes

Severity reflects impact. Reproduction difficulty and visual annoyance do not raise severity by themselves.
